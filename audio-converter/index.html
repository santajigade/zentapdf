Audio Converter changes audio files from one format to another: MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG and FLAC. Pick the output format and quality, add one file or many, and download them one by one or as a ZIP. It also reads less common formats such as WMA, AMR, AIFF and OPUS.

How to convert audio files

  1. Click "Choose audio files" or drag one or more files into the box.
  2. Choose the output format: MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG or FLAC.
  3. For MP3, M4A, AAC and OGG, choose the quality (bitrate). 192 kbps is high quality, 320 kbps is the best.
  4. Click "Convert". Download each file, or all of them as one ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Which format should I choose?

MP3 plays everywhere. M4A (AAC) sounds a little better than MP3 at the same size and is standard on iPhone. WAV and FLAC are lossless: WAV for editing, FLAC for smaller lossless files.

What bitrate should I use?

128 kbps is fine for speech and casual listening, 192 kbps is high quality for music, and 320 kbps is the best MP3 quality.

Does converting improve quality?

No. Converting a low quality MP3 to WAV or FLAC keeps the same sound in a bigger file. Quality can only be kept, not added.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Add as many files as you like; they are converted one after another and you can download them as a ZIP.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is converted in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Why does OGG or FLAC take a moment to start?

Those formats use our audio engine, which is downloaded once (about 10 MB) and then reused.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit. Files up to 100 MB work well on most devices; bigger files work too but need more time and memory.
