Tone Generator plays a steady tone at the exact frequency you choose. Use it to test speakers and headphones, find rattles, tune instruments to A 440 Hz, check the range of your hearing, or create test sounds. Choose the wave shape, volume and left or right channel, and download the tone as WAV or MP3.

How to use the tone generator

  1. Turn your device volume down first, especially with headphones.
  2. Type a frequency in hertz, drag the slider, or pick a preset such as 440 Hz or 1 kHz.
  3. Choose the wave shape: sine for a pure tone, square, sawtooth or triangle for a buzzier sound.
  4. Press Play. Change frequency, volume and balance while it plays.
  5. To save the tone, set the length and download it as WAV or MP3.

Frequently asked questions

What frequency range can humans hear?

Roughly 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz for young people. The upper limit drops with age; many adults cannot hear above 15,000 Hz.

What is 440 Hz?

440 Hz is the note A above middle C, the standard pitch used for tuning instruments.

Can a tone damage my speakers or hearing?

Loud tones can. Start with low volume, avoid very low bass at high volume on small speakers, and do not listen to loud high tones for long.

What is the difference between the wave shapes?

A sine wave is a pure single frequency. Square, sawtooth and triangle waves add overtones, so they sound brighter or buzzier.

How do I test left and right speakers?

Use the Balance slider, or our Speaker Test, which plays sound in each channel.

Can I download the tone?

Yes. Choose a length up to 2 minutes and download it as WAV or MP3.
