PNG and JPG are the two most common image formats, but they are good at different things. Many upload forms, printers and apps accept only JPG, and JPG files are usually much smaller. Here’s how to convert PNG to JPG, and when you should (or shouldn’t).

PNG vs JPG in one minute

PNG JPG
Compression Lossless, every pixel kept Lossy, tiny details simplified
File size for photos Large Small (often 5 to 10× smaller)
Transparency Yes No (becomes white)
Best for Logos, icons, screenshots, graphics Photos, scans, social media, forms

Convert PNG → JPG when: the image is a photo, the file is too big, or a form/app only accepts JPG. Keep PNG when: you need a transparent background (like a logo) or the image is a sharp graphic with text.

How to convert PNG to JPG

  1. Open PNG to JPG.
  2. Click Choose images and select up to 5 PNG files.
  3. Set quality, 90% is a great default. Lower = smaller file.
  4. Click Convert.
  5. Download each JPG, or all of them as a ZIP.

Choosing the right quality

Quality Use it for
90 to 95% Printing, photos you want to keep
75 to 85% Websites, email, WhatsApp, social media
60 to 70% Strict upload limits

Need an exact size like 50 KB for a form? Use Reduce Image Size in KB instead, it finds the best quality automatically.

What about transparency?

JPG can’t store transparent pixels. When you convert a PNG with a transparent background (for example a logo), the transparent part becomes white. If you need to keep transparency but want a smaller file, convert to WebP with PNG to WebP instead.

  • WebP to JPG, images saved from websites are often WebP; convert them so they open everywhere.

Converting on a phone

Open zentapdf.com/png-to-jpg/ in your phone browser, tap Choose images, pick screenshots or photos from your gallery and convert. The JPGs are saved to your downloads.

Converting screenshots

Screenshots are saved as PNG on most phones and computers. They look sharp, but they can be 2 to 5 MB each. Converting to JPG at 85 to 90% usually makes them 70 to 90% smaller while text stays readable. If the text looks fuzzy, raise the quality to 95% or keep the screenshot as PNG.

Converting many images

You can convert up to 5 images at a time. After converting, click Download all (ZIP), then Start over for the next batch. Tip: sort your files into folders of five before you start if you have many.

Common problems and fixes

Problem Why it happens Fix
Transparent logo now has a white box JPG can’t store transparency Use PNG to WebP or keep PNG
JPG is bigger than the PNG Simple graphics compress well as PNG Lower the quality, or keep PNG
Image looks blurry Quality too low Use 90 to 95%
Form still rejects the image File is above the KB limit Use Reduce Image Size in KB
Colours look slightly different JPG compression of solid colour areas Use higher quality for graphics

Does converting remove photo data?

Yes. Hidden information such as camera model and GPS location (EXIF data) is not copied into the new JPG. That’s a nice privacy bonus when you share photos online.

PNG to JPG on Windows and Mac without tools

  • Windows: open the PNG in Paint → File → Save as → JPEG picture.
  • Mac: open in Preview → File → Export → Format: JPEG.

These work for single images. ZentaPDF is faster when you have several images or want to control quality and size.

Frequently asked questions

Does converting PNG to JPG reduce quality?

JPG uses lossy compression, but at 85 to 95% quality the difference is invisible for photos. Screenshots with small text can look slightly softer, so use a high quality setting for those.

What happens to a transparent background?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas become white.

Why is my JPG bigger than the PNG?

Simple graphics with few colours can be smaller as PNG. Lower the quality slider, or keep those images as PNG.

How many images can I convert at once?

Up to 5 images per conversion. Click Start over to convert the next batch.
