You send photos from your iPhone to your computer, and Windows says it can’t open them. Or an online form rejects your photo because it’s a .HEIC file. Here’s what HEIC is and how to convert it to JPG in seconds.

What is HEIC?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the photo format iPhones and iPads use by default. It keeps good quality in roughly half the space of a JPG, great for your phone’s storage, but not every device or website supports it:

Where Opens HEIC?
iPhone, iPad, Mac Yes
Windows PCs Often not, unless extra extensions are installed
Many websites and online forms No, JPG required
Older Android apps Sometimes not

Convert HEIC to JPG online (free)

  1. Open HEIC to JPG.
  2. Choose up to 5 HEIC photos (or drag them in).
  3. Keep quality at 90%, or lower it for smaller files.
  4. Click Convert. The first photo can take a few seconds while the converter loads.
  5. Download each JPG or all of them as a ZIP.

Need lossless output for editing? Use HEIC to PNG.

Make your iPhone save JPG automatically

If you often send photos to forms or Windows PCs, change one setting:

  1. Open Settings → Camera → Formats.
  2. Choose Most Compatible.

New photos are saved as JPG from then on. (Existing photos stay HEIC.)

Also useful: when you share a photo by email or many messaging apps, the iPhone often converts it to JPG automatically.

Using HEIC photos in online forms

Most application forms need JPG under a certain size. The quickest path:

  1. Convert with HEIC to JPG, or go straight to Reduce Image Size in KB, it also accepts HEIC photos and saves JPG.
  2. Set the target size (e.g. 50 KB) and pixel size the form asks for.

Other things you can do with HEIC photos

  • Compress them and save as JPG.
  • Turn them into a PDF with JPG to PDF, useful for scanned documents.
  • Resize or crop them.

Keep HEIC for storage, convert to JPG for sharing, and your iPhone photos work everywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Why are my iPhone photos HEIC?

iPhones save photos in HEIC (High Efficiency) format by default because it uses about half the space of JPG at similar quality.

How do I convert HEIC to JPG on Windows?

Open ZentaPDF HEIC to JPG in Edge or Chrome, choose up to 5 HEIC photos and click Convert. No software installation needed.

Does converting HEIC to JPG lose quality?

At 90% quality the difference is not visible for normal photos. JPG files will be somewhat larger than the HEIC originals.

Can I upload HEIC photos to government or exam forms?

Most forms accept only JPG. Convert the photo to JPG first, then reduce it to the required KB size.
