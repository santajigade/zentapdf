ZIP to PDF Converter

Combine PDFs, images and text files in a ZIP into one PDF.

Choose ZIP file

or drop a ZIP with PDFs, images or text files

ZIP to PDF opens a ZIP archive and combines everything useful inside, PDFs, photos and scans (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC), text and CSV files, into one PDF, in file-name order. Perfect for scanned pages, invoices or documents received as a ZIP.

How to convert a ZIP to PDF

  1. Click "Choose ZIP file" and select a .zip archive.
  2. The files inside are converted and combined automatically, in name order.
  3. Check the preview and the list of skipped files, if any.
  4. Download the combined PDF.

When is ZIP to PDF useful?

Scanned pages, photos of documents and invoices are often shared as one ZIP file, for example from a scanner app, an email attachment or a WhatsApp download. Many upload portals and printers need a single PDF instead. This tool opens the ZIP and turns everything inside into one PDF in one step, so you do not have to unzip, convert and merge the files yourself.

How to make the right page order

Pages are added in file-name order. Name your files 01, 02, 03 and so on before you zip them, and they will appear in that order. Folders inside the ZIP are included too.

After converting

If the PDF is too large for an email or a form, use Compress PDF. To change the order of pages afterwards, use Reorder PDF Pages.

Frequently asked questions

Which files inside the ZIP are used?

PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF and HEIC images, and TXT, CSV, HTML and Markdown files. Other files are skipped and listed.

In what order are pages added?

By file name, with numbers sorted naturally (page2 comes before page10). Rename files before zipping to change the order.

What about password-protected PDFs inside?

Locked PDFs are skipped. Unlock them first with Unlock PDF.

Can I convert a ZIP file to PDF on my phone?

Yes. Open this page in Chrome or Safari on your phone, choose the ZIP file from Downloads or Files, and download the combined PDF.

Does it work with RAR or 7Z files?

No, only .zip files. Most RAR and 7Z tools can save the files again as a ZIP.

Is it really free? Do you add a watermark?

Yes, it is completely free with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limits. The site is supported by a few non-intrusive ads.
