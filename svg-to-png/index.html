Convert SVG to PNG in a few seconds. SVG is a vector format made of shapes instead of pixels, so it stays sharp at any size. Many apps, social media sites and documents do not accept SVG files.

How to convert SVG to PNG

  1. Click "Choose images" or drag and drop up to 5 SVG files into the box.
  2. PNG is lossless, so there is no quality setting to worry about.
  3. Click "Convert".

Frequently asked questions

Why convert SVG to PNG?

PNG keeps perfect quality with no compression artefacts and supports transparent backgrounds, which is what designers, documents and editing apps need.

What happens to transparent backgrounds?

Transparency is kept, PNG supports transparent backgrounds. SVG files are converted at their own size; very small icons stay small.

How many images can I convert at once?

You can convert up to 5 images at a time. When they are done, click "Start over" to convert the next 5.

Yes, it is completely free with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limits. The site is supported by a few non-intrusive ads.
