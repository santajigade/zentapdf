A chronological resume lists your jobs in reverse order, so your latest role comes first and the oldest comes last. Recruiters can see at a glance how your career has grown. This one-page Word template follows that order, with a two-tone header, a short summary, three jobs, education, skills and certifications.

How to use this chronological resume template

  1. Download the Word file and open it in Microsoft Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice Writer.
  2. Replace the name, target job title and contact line in the header. Delete anything you do not want to share.
  3. Write a summary of two or three lines: your role, years of experience and what you are best at.
  4. List your jobs from newest to oldest. For each one, give the job title, company, city and the start and end month and year.
  5. Under each job, write two to four points that start with an action word and include a number where you can.
  6. Fill in education, skills and certifications, then check that everything still fits on one page.
  7. Save as PDF and use a file name like Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf.

What is in the template

The header has a dark band with your name and target role, and a lighter strip under it for phone, email, city and LinkedIn. Then come a professional summary and a work experience section with three jobs for a sample accountant. Each job has its dates on the right and bullet points below.

Below the jobs are education with degree, college and years, a skills block split into accounting, software and languages, and a short certifications list. All of it is ordinary Word text, so applicant tracking systems can read it.

Who should use this format

The chronological format suits people who have stayed in one field and moved up over time, because the list of jobs shows that growth. Most applicant tracking systems read it without trouble.

If you are changing careers, have long gaps or many short jobs, or your recent work does not match the job you want, a skills-based or combination resume may serve you better. Freshers can use our fresher resume template.

Dates, gaps and promotions

Write dates the same way every time, such as Jun 2022 to Present. If you were promoted within a company, give each role its own entry with its own dates. If you have a gap, you can list what you did in that time, such as freelance work, a course or caring for family, as its own short entry.

One page is usually enough with less than about ten years of experience. With more, a second page is fine, as long as the most important work is on the first.

Tips for best results

  • Change your summary and skills for each job you apply for, using words from the job post.
  • Keep older jobs short. Two points are enough for a role from many years ago.
  • Leave out photos, date of birth and marital status unless the employer asks for them.
  • Check spelling, then ask a friend to read it once more.

Frequently asked questions

What does reverse chronological mean?

It means newest first. Your current or most recent job is at the top, and older jobs follow in order.

Is a chronological resume ATS friendly?

This template uses plain text, standard section names and simple tables for layout, which most applicant tracking systems read well. Save it as PDF or Word, whichever the job post asks for.

How far back should my work history go?

Many people show the last 10 to 15 years. Older jobs can be left out or shortened to one line if they do not help with the job you want.

Can I add a fourth job?

Yes. Copy one job block in Word and paste it below. Shorten older points so the resume does not spill onto a second page by accident.

Should education come before experience?

In this format experience comes first. If you graduated recently and have little work history, move education to the top or use our fresher resume template.
