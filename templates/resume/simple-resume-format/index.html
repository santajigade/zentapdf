This simple resume format is a plain, classic one-page layout for people who want a clean resume without colours, photos or graphics. It follows the common reverse chronological order, with your latest job first, and works for office, sales, support, accounts and most other roles.

How to use this simple resume format

  1. Download the Word file and open it in Microsoft Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice.
  2. Replace [Your Full Name], the job title line and your phone, email and city at the top.
  3. Rewrite the Summary in two or three lines: who you are, your years of experience and what you are good at.
  4. Under Work experience, start with your current or latest job. Keep the job title, company, city and dates, then write three or four bullet points for each role.
  5. Add your highest qualification first under Education, then your skills, certifications and languages.
  6. Delete any line you do not need so the resume stays on one page.
  7. Check spelling and dates, save the file as PDF and attach the PDF to your application.

What the template includes

The file is a one-page A4 resume in a serif font with your name at the top and a single contact line under it. It has six short sections: Summary, Work experience with two sample jobs, Education, Skills, Certifications and Languages. Each job shows the title and company on the left and the dates on the right, followed by bullet points.

It is filled with a realistic sample for an operations executive with about five years of experience, so you can see how much to write. Grey text in square brackets marks the details you must replace with your own.

Why a simple format works

Many companies use applicant tracking systems that read your resume before a person does. These systems read plain text, standard headings and simple bullet points best. Text inside images, text boxes or complex designs can be skipped or read in the wrong order. A simple layout also lets a recruiter scan your job titles, companies and dates in a few seconds.

This template uses normal headings such as Work experience and Education, plain bullets and one column for the main content, so it stays easy to read on screen, on paper and in tracking software.

Writing strong bullet points

Start each bullet with an action word such as managed, reduced, trained or handled. Then say what you did and, where you can, add a number: orders per day, money saved, team size or time cut. Two short lines with a result are better than a long list of duties.

Match your words to the job post. If the post asks for vendor management or MS Excel, use those words in your bullets and skills when they are true for you.

Tips for best results

  • Use the same job title words as the job post in your top line when they truly fit your experience.
  • Write dates in one style throughout, such as Mar 2023 to Present.
  • Leave out photo, date of birth and family details unless the employer asks for them.
  • Save the file name as your name, for example Priya-Sharma-Resume.pdf.
  • Keep older jobs to one or two bullet points so space goes to your recent work.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best simple resume format?

For most people the reverse chronological format is best: your latest job first, then older jobs, followed by education and skills. This template uses that order.

Is this resume format ATS-friendly?

Yes. It uses standard section headings, plain bullet points and no images or text boxes, which tracking systems can read easily. Always send the PDF or Word file the job post asks for.

Can freshers use this simple resume format?

Yes. Move Education above Work experience and replace the jobs with internships, projects or part-time work. You can also try the fresher resume template on ZentaPDF.

How do I edit this resume in Google Docs?

Upload the Word file to Google Drive, open it with Google Docs and edit the text. Then choose File, Download, PDF document to save your finished resume.

My resume PDF is too big for the job portal. What can I do?
