Frequently asked questions

Are these templates really free?

Yes. Every template is free to download and use for personal or business work. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on downloads.

Which apps open the files?

Word files open in Microsoft Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice Writer. Excel files open in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. PDF files open in any browser or PDF reader.

Do the Excel templates have working formulas?

Yes. Totals, taxes, differences and dates are calculated with normal Excel formulas. There are no macros, so the files open safely everywhere.

Who designs these templates?

The ZentaPDF team designs every template from scratch. New templates are added every day, so check back often or browse by category.

Can I turn my finished template into a PDF?

Yes. Save it as PDF from Word or Excel, or use the free Word to PDF and Excel to PDF tools on ZentaPDF. You can also sign it with Sign PDF.
