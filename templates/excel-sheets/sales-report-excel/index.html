A monthly sales report shows how much you sold, where it came from and how it compares with last month and with the target. This Excel template has two sheets. The Monthly Report sheet takes sales for up to 10 products across 4 regions, and the Year Trend sheet tracks the 12 months of the year against target.

How to use this sales report excel

  1. Download the Excel file and open it in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice Calc.
  2. On Monthly Report, set the report month, your name and the branch at the top.
  3. Rename the four region headings in row 8 to your own regions, zones or sales people.
  4. Replace the sample products with yours and type each month's sales under each region.
  5. Enter last month's total and this month's target for each product.
  6. Read the four boxes at the top and the comments box, and write a few lines on why sales went up or down.
  7. Copy the Total into the Sales column for that month on the Year Trend sheet, then print or save both sheets as PDF.

What the Monthly Report sheet shows

Each product row has four region columns, a Total, Last month, Change %, Target, Achieved % and Share %. The Total row adds up every column, and the row under it shows each region's share of all sales.

Four boxes at the top show total sales this month, change against last month, target achieved and the best selling product. Below the table you get the best region and how many products are below target. Rows under target turn red in the Achieved % column, and Change % shows rises in green and falls in red.

What the Year Trend sheet shows

The months run from April to March, which suits an Indian financial year. For each month you type sales and target. The sheet then works out achieved %, change against the previous month, and year to date sales, target and achieved %. A small bar in the Achieved % column shows how close each month came to its target.

Making the report useful

Use the comments box to note why the month went the way it did, such as a festival, a price change, a new dealer or a stock shortage, and what you plan to do next month.

Use the same figures every month, for example sales before tax and after returns, so the change % compares like with like. Write down which figure you use in the sub-heading.

Tips for best results

  • Fill the report within the first few days of the new month, while the reasons are still fresh.
  • Use region columns for sales people instead if you want a report by person.
  • Save one file per month and name it with the year and month, for example 2026-11 Sales Report.
  • Set targets you can explain to the team.

Frequently asked questions

What should a monthly sales report include?

Total sales, sales by product and region, comparison with last month and with the target, and a short note on the reasons. This template covers all of these.

How is Change % worked out?

This month's total divided by last month's total, minus 1. A product that sold 1,00,000 last month and 1,10,000 this month shows +10.0%.

Can I add more products or regions?

The sheet holds 10 products and 4 regions. To add products, insert rows above the Total row and copy the formulas from the row above.

Can I track units instead of money?

Yes. Type unit counts in place of amounts. All the formulas work the same, so change the sub-heading to say units.

Does it need macros?

No. It uses only standard formulas and shading rules, so it works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice.
