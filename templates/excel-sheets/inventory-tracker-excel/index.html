This inventory tracker in Excel keeps a stock list and a log of every item that comes in or goes out. Stock on hand, stock value and a reorder status work out by themselves, and items that need reordering turn red. It works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice with no macros.

How to use this inventory tracker excel

  1. Download the Excel file and open it in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice Calc.
  2. On the Stock sheet, replace the sample items with your own: item code, name, category, unit and where it is kept.
  3. Type the opening stock you have today, the reorder level, the usual reorder quantity and the unit cost for each item.
  4. Delete the sample lines on the Moves sheet. Each time stock arrives or leaves, add a line with the date, item code, In or Out, quantity and the bill or PO number.
  5. Check the Stock sheet. The four boxes at the top show the item count, total stock value, items to reorder and items out of stock.
  6. Print the Stock sheet or save it as PDF when you place orders.

What is inside the file

Stock sheet: room for 30 items with item code, name, category, unit, location, opening stock, stock in, stock out, in stock, reorder level, reorder quantity, unit cost, stock value and status. It prints on one landscape page.

Moves sheet: 60 lines for stock movements with date, item code, item name, In or Out, quantity, bill or PO number and notes. The item name fills in from the code, and shows "Code not found" if the code is not on the Stock sheet.

How the formulas work

Stock in and Stock out use SUMIFS. They add up the Quantity column on the Moves sheet for lines with the same item code and the word In or Out. In stock is opening stock plus stock in minus stock out, and stock value is in stock multiplied by unit cost.

Status shows Out of stock when nothing is left, Reorder when stock is at or below the reorder level, and OK otherwise. The whole row turns red for Reorder and Out of stock, so you can see at a glance what to buy.

Setting a sensible reorder level

A common way is to take how many units you sell or use in a day, multiply by the number of days your supplier takes to deliver, then add a small safety stock for busy weeks. For example, if you sell 5 notebooks a day, delivery takes 6 days and you keep 10 spare, the reorder level is 40.

Tips for best results

  • Never edit the Stock in or Stock out cells by hand. Fix mistakes by adding a correcting line on the Moves sheet.
  • Record damaged, expired or free items as Out with a note, so your stock matches the shelf.
  • Count the shelves once a month and compare with the In stock column to catch errors early.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this inventory sheet in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets. The SUMIFS formulas, drop-down lists and red highlights keep working.

How do I add more than 30 items or 60 moves?

Insert new rows above the last row of each table and copy the formulas down from the row above. Inserting inside the range keeps the totals and lookups correct.

Does it track stock value at cost or selling price?

At cost. Stock value is the quantity in stock multiplied by the unit cost you type. If you want selling value too, add a column for selling price and multiply it by In stock.

Can I use it for more than one shop or godown?

Use the Location column for small setups. For separate branches, keep one copy of the file for each place.

Is this the same as a GST stock register?

No. It is a simple stock tracker. Businesses registered under GST may need to keep stock records in a set way, so check the official GST rules or ask your accountant.
