Appointment Letter Format (Word and PDF)

Two-page employer appointment letter with numbered terms of employment and a signed acceptance copy.

An appointment letter is the letter an employer gives a new employee on or around the joining date, setting out the terms of the job. The offer letter comes first and makes the offer; the appointment letter confirms that the person has joined and lists the conditions in full. This two-page Word template has a letterhead, ten numbered terms and an acceptance section the employee signs and returns.

How to use this appointment letter format

  1. Download the Word file and open it in Microsoft Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice Writer.
  2. Put your company name, address and contact details in the letterhead, then the reference number and date.
  3. Fill in the employee's name, address, employee ID, designation, department and date of joining.
  4. Enter the monthly gross salary and annual CTC in the salary table. Attach your salary breakup sheet if you use one.
  5. Go through each numbered term and change the probation, working hours, leave and notice period to match your company policy.
  6. Delete any term that does not apply, and add any that your business needs, such as transfer or non-solicitation.
  7. Print two copies and have the authorised signatory sign both. Give one to the employee and keep the signed acceptance copy in the personnel file.

What is in the template

Page 1 has a letterhead with a rule in the accent colour, a confidential line, reference number and date, the employee's address and a subject line. The opening paragraph confirms the appointment, followed by a small table of employee ID, designation, department, reporting manager, joining date and place of work.

The numbered terms cover the position, salary, probation and confirmation, working hours and leave, notice period and termination, confidentiality and company property, documents and verification, company policies, transfer, and the laws that apply. Page 2 ends with the signatory block and an acceptance section for the employee's signature, date and place.

Appointment letter or offer letter

An offer letter is sent after selection and asks the candidate to accept. The appointment letter comes after acceptance, usually on joining, and is the fuller record of the terms both sides agree to. Many small firms send only one letter; if you do, make sure it has all the terms in this template.

If you still need an offer letter, use our offer letter format first and this letter on the joining day.

Check the rules that apply to you

Employment rules in India depend on the state, the type of establishment, the number of employees and the labour laws and codes in force. Probation length, working hours, leave, notice period, PF, ESI and the rules on issuing appointment letters can all differ.

Use this template as a starting point; it is not legal advice. Check the current central and state rules on official government websites, and have a lawyer or HR advisor read your final letter.

Tips for best results

  • Match the salary, designation and joining date to the offer letter the employee accepted.
  • Use the same notice period for both sides unless your policy says otherwise, and write it in days.
  • Give a reference number to every letter so you can find it later in your records.
  • Ask the employee to sign every page of the acceptance copy, not only the last one.

Frequently asked questions

When should an appointment letter be given?

Most companies give it on the joining day or within the first few days, after the joining documents are checked. Check the labour rules that apply to your business, as some set a time limit.

Is an appointment letter the same as a joining letter?

No. A joining letter is written by the employee to report that they have joined. The appointment letter is written by the employer and sets out the terms.

Does the template include a salary breakup?

It has a small table for monthly gross salary and annual CTC. For a detailed component-wise breakup, attach your own sheet or use the annexure in our offer letter format.

Can I change the terms after the employee signs?

Changes to agreed terms usually need the employee's written consent and a new letter or addendum. Take advice before changing salary, hours or notice terms.

Can I send it by email?

Yes. Save it as PDF, sign it with a PDF signing tool, and ask the employee to return a signed copy of the acceptance page.
