A bonafide certificate is a letter from a school or college confirming that a student is studying there now. Students need one for things like a bank account, a scholarship, a passport application or an education loan. This one-page Word template has the institution header, a student details grid, a purpose line, signature and seal spaces, and a small office record box.

How to use this bonafide certificate format

  1. Download the Word file and open it in Microsoft Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice Writer.
  2. Type the institution's name, address, affiliation and contact details in the header, and put your logo where the template shows. If you print on letterhead, delete the header.
  3. Give the certificate a reference number from your certificate register and add the date of issue.
  4. Fill in the student's name, parent's name and academic year, then the details grid. Take each item from the admission register.
  5. Write the purpose the student asked for, such as a scholarship or a bank account, and delete the options you don't need. Keep or delete the optional conduct line.
  6. Print it, have the principal sign it and stamp the seal, and note the entry in the office record box.

What is in the template

The page has a double green border. The header has the institution's name and details on the left, and the reference number, date and a logo space on the right. A green band below it holds the title Bonafide Certificate with To whom it may concern.

The certifying paragraph names the student, the parent and the academic year. A two-column grid lists enrolment number, class or course, semester, admission date, course duration, date of birth, address on record and hostel or day scholar. Then come the purpose line, an optional conduct line, the place, and spaces for the clerk who prepared it, the seal and the principal. The office record box at the bottom is for the register entry and a contact for anyone who wants to verify the certificate.

Why the purpose line matters

Banks, scholarship offices and passport offices often want the certificate to say what it is for, so the template states the purpose and adds the words "and for no other purpose". If a student needs it for two things, check whether each office wants its own certificate.

How long it stays valid

There is no single rule. Each bank, college or office sets its own limit, and some only accept a certificate issued in the last six months or a year. Ask the office that wants it before the student applies.

Tips for best results

  • Print on the institution's letterhead if you have one.
  • Copy names and dates exactly from the admission register so they match the student's ID and marksheets.
  • Stamp the seal so it touches the principal's signature.
  • Keep a copy, or at least the reference number, in the office file.

Frequently asked questions

Who signs a bonafide certificate?

Usually the principal or head of the institution, and at some universities the registrar. The template has spaces for the principal, the seal and the clerk who prepared it.

How does a student ask for one?

Most institutions want a short application to the principal that gives the purpose. Some have their own form or an online portal.

How is it different from a character certificate?

A bonafide certificate confirms that the student is studying at the institution now. A character certificate is about conduct and is often given when a student leaves. Our character certificate format covers that one.

Can a company use this format for an employee?

Companies issue bonafide certificates for employees too, but the details differ. Replace the student grid with employee ID, designation and joining date, and have HR sign it.

Can I change the details in the grid?

Yes. The grid is a Word table, so you can edit the labels or delete a row you don't need.
